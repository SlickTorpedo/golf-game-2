# Cup Run: the plan

Six big holes, par 5 to 7, about 37 in all. Each is a place in or around the
stadium and is built from sections that each have one idea. This page is the
structure; the builder of each hole fills in the geometry and tunes it.
`docs/hole-design.md` has the rules every hole follows, including the "Big
holes" section.

The round should take a single player about 15 to 20 minutes. Difficulty
rises from hole 1 to hole 5; hole 6 is the showpiece.

Things common to all six:

- Route length (safe line) about 14–16 units per stroke of par.
- Two ways through, and one big shortcut that skips a section at real risk.
- Three to five pickups.
- Mostly walled where the safe line runs; open edges and kerbs mark the risky lines.
- A drop that lands you back on an earlier section is better than a drop into the void.

---

## 1. The Concourse — par 5 — `cuprun/01-concourse.ts`, id `concourse`

The wide walkway under the stand. The gentle opener: big, generous, teaches
that these holes have sections and choices.

1. **Promenade.** Tee on a wide (4) lane that sweeps round a long, gentle
   left-hand bend with boards on the outside. A few pillars (cones) stand in
   the lane. No void.
2. **The fork.** The lane opens into a landing with two exits.
   - Left, short: a bank of **three turnstiles** in a row across a walled
     lane. Time it and you are through in one.
   - Right, long: a ramp that loops round the outside and climbs to the same
     place. No timing, two more putts.
3. **Mezzanine.** Both arrive on a raised deck (height about 1.5) that curls
   round above the green. A kerbed spiral ramp leads down to it.
4. **Green.** Round, boarded, on the low level in the middle of the spiral.

Shortcut: from the mezzanine, hop the kerb on purpose and drop straight onto
the green below. Miss the green and you land on the promenade, far back.

Pickups: one on the promenade, one past the turnstiles, one on the long ramp,
one on the mezzanine edge.

## 2. Car Park — par 6 — `cuprun/02-car-park.ts`, id `car-park`

Flat, wide and full of things to hit. The idea is navigation: there is no
single lane, only a field of obstacles and several ways across it.

1. **Entrance.** Tee in a lane with a **ticket barrier** (one turnstile).
2. **The bays.** A big open deck (about 22 x 16) divided by rows of low walls
   like parking bays: rails with gaps in different places on each row, so the
   way through zig-zags. Speed bumps (long low mounds) cross the aisles and
   turn a rolling ball. Cones mark some gaps. An "oil patch" (ice) sits where
   two aisles meet.
3. **The ramp.** At the far end a ramp climbs in a half-circle to an upper
   deck (height about 1.5), multi-storey style, with boards outside.
4. **Upper deck and bridge.** The upper deck runs back over the bays as a
   bridge to the exit side, and drops by a short ramp to the green.

Shortcut: on the upper deck's approach ramp there is a gap in the wall with a
short kicker. A firm putt jumps from it across to the green's side of the bays
and skips the upper deck entirely. Short, and the ball lands back in the bays.

The outer edge of the bays is kerb only on one side: a ball driven hard across
the deck can leave the car park.

Pickups: two among the bays (one in a dead-end bay), one on the upper deck,
one on the landing side of the jump.

## 3. Floodlight Tower — par 6 — `cuprun/03-floodlight-tower.ts`, id `floodlight-tower`

A climb. A square spiral of ramps and landings round an open middle, four
levels up (height 0 to about 4), with the green on the top platform. Falling
off the inside or outside of the spiral mostly lands you on the level below,
so mistakes cost height, not the ball.

1. **Base.** Tee on a square ground-level deck. The first ramp is wide and walled.
2. **Level 1.** A landing with a **spring pad** in one corner: hit it and the
   ball is thrown up onto level 2, skipping a ramp. Miss and you take the ramp.
3. **Level 2.** The lane narrows (1.6) with a kerb on the inside, over the
   open middle. Running track on the next ramp helps the climb and punishes
   bad aim.
4. **Level 3.** A landing with the mouth of a **tunnel** on a thin spur with
   no walls: it comes out on the top platform beside the green. The safe way
   is one more ramp.
5. **Top.** A round platform with boards on the far side only, and the cup.

Shortcut: the tunnel spur on level 3, and the spring on level 1. Either can be
taken alone.

Make the spiral big enough to read: about 16 x 16 at the base, lanes about 3
wide, ramps rising 1.0 over about 8 units (a resting ball rolls back down a
ramp this steep, so each ramp must be climbed in one putt onto a flat landing).

Pickups: one per level on levels 1 to 3, and one in the open middle at ground
level, reachable only by falling in.

## 4. The Terraces — par 6 — `cuprun/04-terraces.ts`, id `terraces`

A descent. A wide bank of stepped rows, like standing terraces, from a top
concourse (height about 3.5) down to pitch level, where the green is guarded
by a leaking gutter (water).

1. **Top row.** Tee on a long, wide walkway along the top.
2. **The steps.** Six or seven rows, each a long flat tread about 2.4 deep and
   18 or more long, each 0.5 below the last. Dropping row to row is free.
   **Crush barriers** (rails, 3–4 long, staggered from row to row) stand along
   the front edge of the treads, so you cannot simply putt straight down: you
   choose a gap, or hit hard enough to hop a barrier.
   **Aisles** (two ramps cutting down through the rows) are the controlled
   way: slower, safe.
3. **The gutter.** At the bottom, a strip of water runs along the foot of the
   terrace with two narrow crossings (little bridges, 1.4 wide).
4. **Green.** Beyond the gutter, a D-shaped green against a long board.

Shortcut: from the top row, a short strip of running track and a kicker throw
the ball out over several rows and, hit right, over the gutter onto the
green. Wrong, and it is in the water.

The whole bank is wide, so eight players spread out. Ends of the rows: board
at one end, open at the other.

Pickups: on the third row, in the middle of the fifth row behind a barrier,
on one of the gutter crossings.

## 5. Groundsman's Yard — par 7 — `cuprun/05-groundsmans-yard.ts`, id `groundsmans-yard`

The technical hole. A sequence of four short, different problems, each
something from the groundsman's shed, joined by landings. The hardest hole.

1. **Line marker.** From the tee, the safe lane curves round, but straight
   ahead lie three thin white lines (lanes 1.1 wide, no walls, 5–6 long each)
   fanning out across a gap to the next landing. Pick one and putt true.
2. **Sprinklers.** A wide lawn dotted with round ponds of water (the
   sprinklers have been left on) and low mounds between them. Many paths, none
   straight.
3. **The roller.** A long straight of running track down a gentle slope with a
   sharp banked bend at the bottom, boards outside. Too fast and you are over
   the kerb on the inside of the next section; the skill is feeding the ball
   onto it gently.
4. **The shed.** A walled room with two tunnel mouths side by side. One comes
   out beside the green. The other comes out back on the sprinkler lawn. They
   are told apart only by the colour of their rings (blue in, orange out, so
   the player must look where each orange exit is).
5. **Green.** Small, with a patch of frost (ice) across the front and mud behind.

Shortcut: line marker's middle line is the longest and leads past the
sprinkler lawn straight to the top of the roller.

Pickups: at the far end of one line, in the middle of the lawn on a mound, in
the shed, before the green.

## 6. Lap of Honour — par 7 — `cuprun/06-lap-of-honour.ts`, id `lap-of-honour`

The finale: the running track itself. An oval circuit (about 44 x 26 outside,
lane 4 wide) with banked bends, round an infield that holds the green. You
start on the home straight and must get the ball to the infield, either by
running the lap or by cutting across.

1. **Home straight.** Tee at the start line. Strips of running track (boost)
   along the straight, with gaps of turf between so a ball can also be stopped.
2. **First bend.** A banked 180° bend, board outside, kerb inside. A ball with
   pace rides the board round.
3. **Back straight.** Hurdles: three low kerbs across the lane (a firm putt
   hops each; a soft one stops at it). A lane on the inside edge goes round
   them slowly.
4. **Second bend and the gate.** A second banked bend brings you back toward
   the start; at its exit a ramp leads down into the infield.
5. **Infield.** The middle of the oval, lower than the track (height about
   -1 relative to it): a wide green with the cup in the centre circle.

Shortcuts, both from the track into the infield over its inside kerb:
- **Long jump.** Off the home straight: a kicker, then a sand pit (mud) in the
  infield that stops the ball dead short of the green. Safe but slow.
- **Water jump.** Off the back straight after the hurdles: a kicker over a
  strip of water straight onto the green. Short is wet.

Dropping into the infield anywhere over the inside kerb is allowed and lands
on grass; the green is small enough that this still leaves a long putt.

Pickups: on the home straight between boost strips, on the outside of the
first bend, after the hurdles, in the sand pit.
